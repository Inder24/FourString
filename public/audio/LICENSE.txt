Ukulele sample recordings from FreePats ukulele1
https://github.com/freepats/ukulele1

Recorded by Mateusz Dabrowski from a Flight Fireball tenor ukulele.
Published under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 public domain dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source repository includes thirteen pitched recordings plus body-tap and
muted-chuck articulations. These files are bundled locally so the instrument
does not depend on an audio CDN at runtime.
