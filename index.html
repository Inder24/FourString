How to play

Hold. Then strum.

On touch, keep your fingers on the neck. Tap the body to play all four strings together, or sweep for an ordered strum.

One-hand touch latches frets so you can strum afterward. Two-hand touch holds the neck while another finger plays. Fingerpick previews notes and builds a phrase.
